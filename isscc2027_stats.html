    2. Academia vs industry

    Each affiliation is classified as academia (universities), research institute (CAS institutes, imec, CSEM, national labs, etc.) or industry. A paper is academia only if every author is from a university or research institute, industry only if every author is from a company, and collaboration otherwise. Research institutes are grouped with academia in the three-way split.

    5. Sessions

    Method and caveats

    • Source: ISSCC 2027 Advance Program PDF. 278 regular papers parsed from Sessions 2–40 (Session 1 plenary invited talks excluded).
    • Not included because the program lists “papers to come”: Session 26 (Late Breaking Industry Highlights) and the two Demonstration Sessions. Forum, tutorial, short-course and evening-event speakers are also excluded.
    • Region and institution come from the printed affiliation. Footnotes such as “now at …” are ignored. Multi-site companies (e.g., Marvell in the US/Canada/Singapore) are counted by the site printed for each author; the “first-author region” of a paper is the site of the first author.
    • Institution names are merged where one organization appears under several labels (e.g., TSMC and its Corporate Research / Design Technology units, UM Hetao Institute with University of Macau, BNRist with Tsinghua). Institution classification was done with keyword rules plus manual overrides and may contain a few edge-case errors (for example, joint institutes and university-affiliated labs).
    • Technology-node and topic keywords, where mentioned, are taken from paper titles only.
